Smarter, lighter, more automatic:
• Public Wi-Fi guard: on a Wi-Fi with no password or a login page
  (hotel, airport, café), your "Hide my IP" tunnel turns on by itself
  after you log in, and off again when you leave. Only if you've saved
  a tunnel config; can be switched off in Hide my IP
• Save mobile data: tap an app → "Wi-Fi only" keeps it offline on mobile
  data; on Wi-Fi it works normally
• New-app report: a day after you install an app, one quiet note says how
  many trackers it tried to contact and from which companies
• Battery: Noxa's helper threads now sleep until there's work (before,
  they woke several times a second all day), and the notification and
  widget only redraw when the count changes and the screen is on
• "Don't filter this app" applies instantly, no off/on
