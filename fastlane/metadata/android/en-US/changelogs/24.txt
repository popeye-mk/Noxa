New: "Report to my home server" (off by default, under More settings). Switch it on and Noxa sends a short hourly report to a server in your own house — protection on/off, blocked counts by category, scam and spyware warnings — so one screen at home shows every phone, tablet and TV. Only on your home Wi-Fi, never the sites you visit. Format and a tiny example server: docs/HOME-SERVER.md.
