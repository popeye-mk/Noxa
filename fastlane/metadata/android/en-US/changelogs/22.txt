Three more shields, all automatic or one tap away:
• Check a link: long-press any link (SMS, WhatsApp, email) → Share →
  "Check link with Noxa" → instant answer: fake PayPal/bank sites,
  scam and spyware sites, disguised links, shortened links, risky
  endings. Offline — the link never leaves your phone
• Spy-app watch: once a day Noxa checks for apps that can read and
  control your screen AND hide from uninstalling (or weren't installed
  from an app store) — how banking trojans and stalkerware work. You
  only hear about it if one is found
• Router attack shield: websites can no longer point ordinary-looking
  names at your router, camera or printer (DNS rebinding). Home names
  (fritz.box, .local, Plex) still work; it stands aside on hotel Wi-Fi
  until you've logged in
Also in this update (from 1.12): public Wi-Fi guard, "Wi-Fi only" apps,
new-app reports and big battery savings.
