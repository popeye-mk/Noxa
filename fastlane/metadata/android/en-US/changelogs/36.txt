1.16.3 — the public Wi-Fi guard works again with the family filter on

• 1.16.2 stopped the public Wi-Fi guard on any phone with the family filter or safe search on. It now only rests on a device with a parent lock or bedtime; the tunnel keeps adult sites blocked anyway.
• A tracker is no longer called a "scam site": well-known analytics companies stay blocked without the dangerous-site warning.
