Safety and staying up to date:
• NEW spyware alert: if any app tries to contact a known stalkerware
  server, Noxa blocks it AND warns you, naming the app
• NEW "a newer Noxa is available" notice, once a day, with a link to the
  download page (Noxa never installs anything by itself)
• Settings & tools → "Check for a new Noxa version"
• Releases are now built and signed on GitHub, so updates ship faster
• NEW choose who answers your lookups: Cloudflare (default), Quad9,
  Mullvad, AdGuard, Google, or your own server — applies instantly
• Fixed: apps are now named correctly on phones that use IPv6 DNS
  (used to show as "system / unknown")
• NEW dangerous-site alert: when a browser hits a known scam, phishing or
  malware site, Noxa blocks it AND tells you not to enter passwords there;
  a non-browser app doing it gets a stronger warning
• NEW Protection check-up: what keeps you protected, what's missing, and a
  one-tap fix for each (battery, Always-on, alerts, encryption, list age)
• Protection now restarts itself within seconds if it ever dies, and tells
  you if it can't
