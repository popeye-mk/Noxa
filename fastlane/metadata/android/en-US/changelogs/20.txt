Noxa looks after itself:
• The check-up runs by itself: you get one notification only if something
  breaks (Always-on switched off, the phone keeps stopping Noxa, the list
  stops updating) — no need to open it to find out
• Battery check judged by what really happens: after 3 days without an
  unexpected stop it's marked OK automatically (works on Xiaomi too)
• If your DNS provider stops answering, Noxa switches to the next one for
  a few minutes by itself, then goes back to your choice
• Fix an app that won't work: the apps Noxa just blocked something for are
  listed first, a gentle fix (allow only what it was blocked from) comes
  before bypassing, and changes apply instantly — no off/on
• DNS provider: "Test speed" times each provider on your network and marks
  the fastest
• Shorter menu: list and app updates are fully automatic now
• If Noxa blocks an app's own server and nothing else gets through, it
  offers to fix it — once a week at most, never for system apps
• Pop-up ad apps are looked for automatically once a day; you only hear
  about it if one is found
• Sunday evening: a short weekly summary of what Noxa blocked
• Clearer names in your stats (Datadog, Sift, Criteo and more)
