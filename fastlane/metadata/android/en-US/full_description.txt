Privacy is a right, not a privilege.

Noxa blocks ads, trackers, and malware across your WHOLE phone — every app, not just the browser. One switch, that's the whole setup.

How it works: Noxa runs a local filter on Android's VpnService (it is not a real VPN by default — nothing leaves your phone). Every app's DNS lookups are checked against a compiled filter of ~900,000 known ad, tracker, malware, and phishing domains. Blocked domains simply never connect.

Features:

• System-wide blocking — every app, every browser, no configuration
• Plain-language stats — see WHO is tracking you ("Google · Advertising", "Xiaomi · Analytics"), per app
• Per-app firewall — block any app from the internet entirely with one switch
• CNAME uncloaking — catches trackers disguised as first-party subdomains
• Secure-DNS bypass protection — browsers can't tunnel their lookups around the filter
• Encrypted DNS (DoH) — hides your lookups from your ISP or Wi-Fi
• Hide my IP — optional WireGuard tunnel through YOUR provider (Proton, Mullvad, IVPN, or your own server), with blocking active while tunnelling
• Your own allowlist — un-block anything, yourself
• 30-day rolling stats, CSV export

The guarantees:

• Zero telemetry — nothing about you leaves your device, verifiable in the source
• No servers — Noxa has no backend, so there is no bill that could ever force it to monetize you
• No location permission, no accounts, no ads
• Free forever — AGPL-3.0 copyleft: nobody, not even the author, can close it and sell it

Honest limits: DNS-level blocking cannot hide same-domain "cosmetic" ads (pair Noxa with a privacy browser for those), and it cannot stop a service you are logged into from tracking what you do on that service.

Built for the people, and given back to them.
